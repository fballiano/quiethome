QuietHome is a quiet home screen for Android TV and Google TV. It shows your apps, your HDMI inputs, a photograph and the time. It has no rows of suggestions and no ads. It is made for the TV remote.

WHAT IT DOES

• List or Dock layout. Show names, icons or both. Show a 24-hour or 12-hour clock. You can also show the date under the clock.
• One-colour icons. Known services use a built-in mark. For every other app, QuietHome turns the app's own icon into a mark in the same colour.
• Backgrounds. Choose one of 15 photographs, a light colour or a dark colour. Photos can rotate each time Home opens, or every 5, 15 or 60 minutes.
• All apps. It shows every TV app and every HDMI input. Hold OK on an app to close it and free its memory.
• Your own order. Hold OK on an app on Home to move it or to remove it. You can put up to nine apps on Home.
• Input names. Give an HDMI input a name that you know, for example "Game console".
• Typography and focus. Choose Manrope, Geist or Newsreader. Choose Type focus or Glass focus, with a Soft, Frost or Clear material.
• Your language. QuietHome uses the language of the TV, in 14 languages.

THE QUIETHOME SERVICE

QuietHome has an accessibility service. It is off until you turn it on. Before you turn it on, QuietHome shows what the service does, and you select Agree and open settings.

When the service is on:
• The Home button always opens QuietHome, also on TVs that keep their own home screen.
• You can give a remote button a new job: open an app or an HDMI input, open QuietHome or the TV settings, or do nothing.
• When the TV turns on, QuietHome can open an HDMI input or an app.

The service does not collect data. It checks each key press and each app that comes to the front on the TV, at that moment, and then it forgets. It does not keep or send anything. It cannot read the screen.

PRIVACY

QuietHome does not collect data. It does not use the network. It has no accounts, no analytics and no ads.
